Defensive Capabilities

Engineered for real-world scenarios.

Most privacy issues happen in physical spaces: public trains, busy cafes, or crowded streets. Phone Anti Snatcher provides immediate physical countermeasures.

Instant Privacy Curtain

Activate an immediate stealth blackout overlay across your screen. Trigger it via a quick settings tile or a hardware volume button double-press to instantly conceal private chats, banking apps, and sensitive notes from shoulder surfers.

Anti-Snatch Motion Sentinel

Utilizes on-device tri-axial accelerometer math to continuously analyze motion jerk dynamics. If an aggressive sudden grab or snatch force is detected, the device locks down instantly, blanks the display, and mutes audio output.

Fort Knox Anti-Tamper System

Thieves often try to pull down the Android quick-settings shade immediately after a snatch to toggle Airplane mode or disable location services. Our anti-tamper interceptor blocks shade expansions and forces instant lockdown.

Secret Morse Tap & PIN

Unlock your blackout screen without looking. In addition to a master 4-digit PIN, configure a custom rhythm tap sequence to disarm the shield discreetly with subtle finger taps on the blank glass.

Hardware Volume Shortcut

Double-press Volume Down to instantly black out or wake your privacy screen without having to look at your phone or unlock your device. Ideal for subtle concealment when someone walks up to your desk or seat.

100% On-Device & Offline

Anti Phone Snatcher requests zero internet permissions. There are no tracking SDKs, no analytics relays, and no remote servers. Your data and phone activity remain exclusively on your physical hardware.
